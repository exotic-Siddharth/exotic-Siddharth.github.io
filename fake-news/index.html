How it works

  • TF-IDF turns the text into numbers: each of the 10,000 most common training words gets a score that is higher when it appears often in this article but rarely across all articles.
  • Logistic Regression gives every word a weight. Positive weights point to real news, negative weights to fake news, and the total becomes a probability.
  • Trained on 44,898 articles; 0.98 accuracy on a held-out 20% split. The weights were exported from the trained scikit-learn model to this page and give the same probabilities (checked on 300 test articles).

Limitations (worth trying!)

  • Source shortcut: 98.9% of the real articles in the training data contain a (Reuters) dateline, so the model partly learned “sounds like Reuters = real”. Try the first two examples above: the only difference is that one word, and the confidence changes.
  • It judges writing style, not facts. A well-written false story can score as real, and a sloppy true one as fake.
  • The training articles are political and world news from 2015–2018, so other topics and recent events are less reliable.
